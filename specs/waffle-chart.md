# Waffle chart

A grid where each cell is a share of a whole: 10 × 10 by default, so a cell is
1%. One number ("37% answered") or up to eight parts. A CSS grid of plain
elements; the whole grid is one image with a text summary, not a hundred
announced cells.

## Anatomy

```
<div x-data x-ds-chart data-ds-chart-axis="both" class="relative flex …">     ← x-ds-chart only when `interactive`
    <div data-ds-chart-plot role="img" aria-label="…summary…" class="grid gap-0.5"
         style="grid-template-columns: repeat(10, max-content)">
        <span class="size-3.5 rounded-[2px] bg-current text-series-1" data-ds-chart-mark aria-label="Free: 62 (62%)"></span>
        <span class="… text-series-1" data-ds-chart-piece="0"></span>        ← the rest of the part's cells
        …
        <span class="… text-fg/10"></span>                                  ← empty cells
    </div>
    <ul role="list">legend: swatch, name, "62 · 62%"</ul>
    <table class="sr-only">Part, Value, Share, and the total</table>
    <div data-ds-chart-readout hidden aria-hidden="true">…</div>
</div>
```

| Part | Token | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cells | `series-1` … `series-8` by position; a row's `tone` or `series`; `tone` for one number | specs/chart-series.md |
| Empty cells | the progress track, `fg/10` | |
| Cell | `sm` 10, `md` 14, `lg` 18px; `square` (2px corners) or `circle` | |
| Gap | 2px | |

## Cells

`Chart::cells()` shares rows × columns out by **largest remainder**, the empty
remainder taking part, so the counts always total the grid. A part above zero
that would round to no cell gets one, taken from the part with the most: a
part the legend lists and the grid does not show reads as a mistake, and the
cost is at most a cell. The printed percentages come from
`Chart::percentages()` against the whole, `<1%` for a part too small to round
to one, and on a 10 × 10 grid they equal the cell counts.

Cells are emitted in reading order, each part's run and then the empty ones,
and the grid places them: in a right-to-left page it fills from the right with
nothing in the view knowing. The view does not force a direction.

## Data

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| a number | one part, named by `label`, against `total` (default 100) |
| parts (`['Free' => 62, …]` or rows with `label`, `value`, `tone` / `series`) | against `total`, default their sum |
| `total` smaller than the sum | ignored: the whole is the sum |
| `rows` / `columns` | 1 to 20 each, clamped |
| a negative or a non-number | **throws**, naming the part |
| more than eight parts | **throws** |
| no parts, or a whole of zero | `ds::empty-state`, bare |

## The summary

One number: "Answered: 37 of 100 (37%)." Parts, generated by
`Chart::whole()` as the split bar's: the gist with a legend, every part
without one. `summary` replaces it; `table` adds part, value and share, and
the total.

## Accessibility

| | Without the script | With it |
|---|---|---|
| Role | `img` on the grid | `group`, keeping the name; each part's first cell `img`, "Pro: 30 (30%)" |
| Keyboard | none | one Tab stop; any arrow moves part to part (never cell to cell), Home / End, Escape |
| Pointer | — | over any cell of a part: its readout, beside that cell (`data-ds-chart-piece` names the part) |
| Focus ring | — | on the part's first cell |

The cells carry no names and no roles of their own, so a screen reader meets
one image, or one stop per part.

## Do not

- **Do not use it for precise comparison.** Counting cells is slow; it shows
  a share at a glance. Bars compare.
- **Do not use more than a handful of parts**, or parts under a cell each.
- **Do not change the grid to make a number fit**: 10 × 10 is what makes a
  cell read as 1%. Use another shape only when the whole has a natural count
  (5 × 20 for 200 invitations, 2 to a cell).
